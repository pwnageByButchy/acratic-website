What we do

Cyber security training

Acratic Educational Services runs a five-day Cyber Engineering Bootcamp with tracks for systems, software, hardware and management roles. It also offers Intelligence Analysis courses for practitioners entering government, law enforcement, defence and private-sector intelligence work.

Offensive security research

Authorised penetration testing and vulnerability research, plus deliberately vulnerable lab environments built for training.

Intelligence and research tooling

Acratic² is an OSINT and case management platform for Australian intelligence professionals, including an authorised offensive security module. Acratic Search gives fast search and retrieval across research wikis without a database or external services.

Surveillance and sentry platforms

WatchTower is a video surveillance and intelligence platform that detects and re-identifies people, vehicles and objects and keeps the results as searchable intelligence. Rover is a patrol-grade quadruped sentry that gives a site full interior coverage without fixed indoor cameras, with recognition decisions made by WatchTower.

Security engineering

Hardened infrastructure built as code, and SIEM design and operation.

Community initiative

Acratic Professional Services supports Study Sessions with Butchy, a community initiative running since 2018 for anyone entering security who asks "where do I start?". A volunteer team of fifteen experienced industry professionals has worked with more than 100 students, IT professionals and enthusiasts, working through one educational resource per session as a group and building offensive and defensive security skills from the ground up.
